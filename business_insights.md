# Business Insights

These insights are calculated from the cleaned 51,271-record dataset used for the project.

- Total sales were approximately **$12.64M**, with total profit of approximately **$1.47M**.
- **APAC** was the highest-sales market at approximately **$3.58M**.
- **Central** was the highest-sales region at approximately **$2.82M**.
- **Technology** recorded the highest average profit per record among the three categories.
- **Furniture** had the highest average discount at approximately **16.80%**.
- **Standard Class** was the most frequently used shipping mode.
- **Copiers** had the highest total profit among sub-categories.
- The highest-sales month was **November 2014**, with approximately **$555K** in sales.
- There were **12,538 negative-profit records** in the cleaned dataset.

These results are intended as portfolio-level business insights and should be interpreted in the context of the dataset.
